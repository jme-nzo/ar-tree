A tree that grows when you look

Two builds of the same artwork. Both run in the browser on iOS and Android, no app. Open either one on your phone.

Start here · nothing to print Free mode The tree stands in front of you. Move the phone to look around it. Works immediately. The real mechanic · needs a target Marker mode The tree grows out of a printed image. Point the camera at the target below, or compile your own QR code as the target. Marker mode needs this The test target Open this on a laptop screen and point your phone at it. Later you replace it with your own QR code. When you are ready Compile your own target Upload your QR code image, download the .mind file, drop it in the repo.

Tap the scan counter inside either mode to open the growth slider. Drag it from 0 to 400 to see every stage of the tree.
